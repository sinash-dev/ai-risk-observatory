# Deployer pack for high-risk AI (EU AI Act Art. 26)

For organisations that **use** a high-risk AI system built by someone else. One copy per system. Each line is a duty from Art. 26, with room for the evidence that shows you meet it. Art. 26 was not changed by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. For systems listed in Annex III these duties apply from 2 December 2027, and for systems covered by Annex I from 2 August 2028 (Art. 113).

> **Careful:** you are treated as the provider, with the provider's much longer list of duties, if you put your own name or trademark on a high-risk system, make a substantial modification to it, or change the intended purpose of an AI system so that it becomes high-risk (Art. 25(1)).

## Fundamental-rights impact assessment (Art. 27)
Needed before first use if we are a body governed by public law, a private entity providing public services, or we use the system for credit scoring or for risk assessment and pricing in life and health insurance (Annex III, points 5(b) and (c)).
