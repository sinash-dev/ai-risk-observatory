# Workshop facilitator guide: AI risk in 60 minutes

For mixed groups of managers, lawyers and engineers (8–30 people). You need a screen and, ideally, laptops in groups of 3–4.

## Before the session
- [ ] Test the links below on the room's network.
- [ ] Decide which framework matters most to the group (e.g. EU AI Act).
- [ ] Print the human oversight checklist and the risk assessment template (optional).

## Plan
| Time | Activity | Link |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | **Warm-up.** Open the home page. Each group places three AI systems they use at work on the rings. Read section 2 of The basics together. | https://sinash-dev.github.io/ai-risk-observatory/basics/ |
| 10–25 min | **Break and fix.** Open credit scoring with the postcode problem already on. Watch approvals change, then find the safeguard. Each group reports one surprise. | https://sinash-dev.github.io/ai-risk-observatory/model/credit/?p=zip |
| 25–40 min | **One framework.** Set "View through" to the chosen framework. Follow the first four steps of its learning path. | https://sinash-dev.github.io/ai-risk-observatory/rules/ |
| 40–50 min | **Compare.** Pick Transparency. Where would we comply in one country and not another? Try the gap finder. | https://sinash-dev.github.io/ai-risk-observatory/compare/ |
| 50–55 min | **The hard case.** Open emotion recognition with staff scoring on. What do you do when no safeguard works? | https://sinash-dev.github.io/ai-risk-observatory/model/emotion/?p=rank |
| 55–60 min | **Take away.** Each person names one system at work, its likely category and one safeguard to check on Monday. | — |

## Debrief questions
1. Which problem surprised you most, and would your organisation notice it?
2. Who in your organisation would have to accept the residual risk?
3. Which safeguard would be hardest to fund, and why?
4. Where do two frameworks ask for different things, and how would you handle both?

## Follow-up
- Ask participants to take a quiz and print their certificate as a record of AI-literacy activity (EU AI Act Art. 4): https://sinash-dev.github.io/ai-risk-observatory/certificate/
- Share the "Before go-live" checklist from the example closest to their work.
