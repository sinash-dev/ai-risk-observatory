# Quality management system outline (EU AI Act Art. 17)

For **providers** of high-risk AI systems. The system must be documented "in a systematic and orderly manner" as written policies, procedures and instructions, and cover at least the 13 aspects of Art. 17(1), points (a) to (m). Use this outline to map each aspect to the document that covers it, and to spot gaps.

> **Proportionate to your size.** Since Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Art. 17(2) says the aspects must be implemented in proportion to the size of the provider, in particular for SMEs, start-ups and small mid-cap companies. The rigour and level of protection needed for compliance stay the same.

> **Harmonised standard.** EN 18286:2026 covers quality management systems for the AI Act. Its reference was published in the Official Journal by Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274, so following it gives a presumption of conformity with the requirements it covers. Using it is voluntary. The standard is sold by national standards bodies; this outline does not reproduce it.

> **Existing systems.** If you already run a quality management system under sector law (for example for medical devices), you may include these aspects in it (Art. 17(3)). Financial institutions covered by EU internal-governance rules meet most of Art. 17 through those rules, except points (g), (h) and (i) (Art. 17(4)).
