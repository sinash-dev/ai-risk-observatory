# NYC Local Law 144: candidate notice and bias audit summary

For employers and employment agencies that use an **automated employment decision tool** (AEDT) to screen or assess candidates for hiring or employees for promotion in New York City. Enforced by the NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP) since 5 July 2023.

**It applies when** the job is located in an NYC office (at least part time), the job is fully remote but tied to an NYC office, or the employment agency is in NYC. It does not cover tools used only to scan a résumé bank, reach out to potential candidates or invite applications.

## Checklist
- [ ] **Bias audit done within the past year** by an independent auditor, before using the tool. Each audit can be relied on for one year (DCWP FAQ II.4).
- [ ] The auditor is independent: not employed by us or the vendor, not involved in using, developing or distributing the tool, and without a direct or material indirect financial interest in us or the vendor (DCWP FAQ IV.2).
- [ ] **Summary of results published** on the employment section of our website, or linked from it, with the tool's distribution date (DCWP FAQ II.3).
- [ ] **Notice given at least 10 business days before use** to candidates and employees who live in NYC (Admin. Code § 20-871(b); DCWP FAQ VI.1).
- [ ] **Data information** (type of data, its source and our retention policy) published on our website, or provided within 30 days of a written request (§ 20-871(b)(2)).
- [ ] Results reviewed against anti-discrimination law. LL 144 itself does not require any action based on the results, but federal, state and city anti-discrimination laws still apply (DCWP FAQ II.2).

Penalties: up to $500 for a first violation and each further violation on the same day, then $500 to $1,500 for each later violation. Each day the tool is used without meeting the rules counts as a separate violation (§ 20-872).

## 1. Candidate notice
Give it in the job posting or by mail or email. For applicants you may instead post it on the employment section of your website (it need not be for a specific job); for promotion candidates you may put it in a written policy or procedure. Start using the tool no earlier than 10 business days after the notice.

> **Use of an automated employment decision tool**
> [Organisation] uses an automated employment decision tool, [tool name], to help [screen / assess] [candidates for this position / employees for promotion]. The tool assesses the following job qualifications and characteristics: [list, for example relevant experience, skills listed in the job description, answers to the screening questions].
> **Accommodation:** if you need a reasonable accommodation under applicable law, contact [contact] and we will tell you how to request one.
> **Data:** information about the type of data collected for the tool, its source and our data retention policy is available at [link], or on written request to [contact]. We reply within 30 days.

> Number of people assessed who fall in an unknown category: [number]
