# Vendor AI questionnaire

Send to suppliers of AI systems, models or AI features. Ask for evidence, not just answers.

**Vendor:** [name] · **Product:** [name, version] · **Our use case:** [describe] · **Date:** [date]

## 1. Purpose and classification
1. What is the intended purpose, and which uses do you exclude?
2. How do you classify the product under the EU AI Act, and what is your role (provider)?
3. If high-risk: is there a CE marking, EU declaration of conformity and registration? [attach]

## 2. Model and data
4. What model(s) does it use, including third-party foundation models?
5. What data was it trained on? Was personal data used, and on what basis?
6. How did you examine data for bias and gaps?

## 3. Performance and testing
7. What accuracy metrics do you publish, overall and by relevant group?
8. What robustness and security testing has been done (e.g. against the OWASP lists)? [attach reports]

## 4. Transparency and oversight
9. What instructions for use and documentation do you provide (Art. 13)?
10. What tools support human oversight, explanations and logs?

## 5. Operations
11. How are model updates announced and tested before release?
12. How do you monitor performance and report incidents to customers?
13. Where is data processed and stored? Is our data used to train your models? [yes / no]

## 6. Certifications and contracts
14. Do you hold ISO/IEC 42001 or ISO/IEC 27001 certification? [attach]
15. Will you sign a data processing agreement and accept audit rights?

**Reviewed by:** [name] · **Outcome:** [approve / approve with conditions / reject]
