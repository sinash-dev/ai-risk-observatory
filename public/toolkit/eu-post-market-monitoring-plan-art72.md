# Post-market monitoring plan (EU AI Act Art. 72)

For **providers** of high-risk AI systems. The post-market monitoring system must actively and systematically collect, document and analyse data on how the system performs throughout its lifetime, so you can check it keeps meeting the requirements (Art. 72(1) and (2)). It must be based on a plan, and the plan is part of the technical documentation (Art. 72(3); Annex IV, point 9).

> **No official template yet.** Since Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Commission must adopt guidance, including a template, by 2 September 2027 (Art. 72(3)). This draft follows the text of the Act. Switch to the Commission's template once it is published.

> **Already monitoring under other law?** For products under Annex I, Section A, and for Annex III point 5 systems of financial institutions, you may build these elements into the monitoring system you already run, if the protection is equivalent (Art. 72(4)).

## 5. Serious incidents (Art. 73)
Report to the market surveillance authority of each Member State where the incident happened, immediately once you establish a causal link or a reasonable likelihood of one, and at the latest:

| Case | Deadline after becoming aware |
|---|---|
| Serious incident (general) | 15 days (Art. 73(2)) |
| Widespread infringement, or serious and irreversible disruption of critical infrastructure (Art. 3(49)(b)) | 2 days (Art. 73(3)) |
| Death of a person | 10 days (Art. 73(4)) |

- An incomplete first report is allowed, followed by a full one (Art. 73(5)).
- Do not change the system in a way that could affect the investigation before telling the authorities (Art. 73(6)).
