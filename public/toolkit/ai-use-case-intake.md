## 2. People affected

- **Could anyone be harmed if it is wrong? How?** [describe]

## 3. Quick screening
- [ ] Does it do anything on the EU AI Act banned list (Art. 5)? (If yes, stop.)
- [ ] Is it in a high-risk area (Annex III) or part of a regulated product (Annex I)?
- [ ] Is it a general-purpose AI model we develop?
- [ ] Does it talk to people or generate content (transparency duties)?
- [ ] Does it make significant decisions without meaningful human involvement (GDPR Art. 22)?
- [ ] Does it act on its own with tools such as payments, email or code?

Tip: run the answers through the "Classify your system" page.
