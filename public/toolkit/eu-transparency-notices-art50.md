# Transparency notices (EU AI Act Art. 50)

Sample wording for the four transparency duties in Art. 50. They apply from 2 August 2026, whatever the risk level of the system (Art. 113). Providers of generative systems already on the market before that date have until 2 December 2026 to add machine-readable marking (Art. 111(4)). Adapt the wording to your product, language and audience.

The information must be clear and distinguishable, given at the latest at the first interaction or exposure, and meet accessibility requirements (Art. 50(5)). These duties are in addition to any others under EU or national law, such as GDPR information duties (Art. 50(6)).

| Art. 50 | Who | Duty | Exceptions in the Act |
|---|---|---|---|
| (1) | Provider | Design systems that interact with people so those people are told they are dealing with an AI | When it is obvious to a reasonably well-informed, observant and circumspect person; systems authorised by law for criminal investigation, unless the public can use them to report a crime |
| (2) | Provider | Mark synthetic audio, image, video or text output in a machine-readable format, detectable as AI-generated or manipulated | Assistive function for standard editing; no substantial change to the input or its meaning; authorised by law for criminal investigation |
| (3) | Deployer | Tell people exposed to emotion recognition or biometric categorisation that it is operating | Uses permitted by law to detect, prevent or investigate crime |
| (4) | Deployer | Disclose deepfakes; disclose AI-generated or manipulated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest | Artistic, creative, satirical or fictional works: disclose in a way that does not spoil the work. Text: not needed if a person reviewed or edited it and someone holds editorial responsibility |

Emotion recognition at work or in education is banned outright, except for medical or safety reasons (Art. 5(1)(f)). A notice does not make it lawful.

## 1. Chatbot or voice assistant (Art. 50(1))
**At the start of the conversation, before the first reply:**
> You are chatting with [name], an AI assistant. It can make mistakes. [To talk to a person, type "agent" / call ___.]

**Voice:**
> This call is answered by an automated AI assistant. Say "person" at any time to speak to a member of staff.

## 3. Emotion recognition or biometric categorisation (Art. 50(3))
**Before people are exposed, for example on a sign or screen at the entrance:**
> This [area / service] uses an AI system that [analyses facial expressions to estimate emotions / sorts people into groups based on biometric data] for [purpose]. Personal data is processed as described in our privacy notice: [link]. Contact: [contact].

## 4. Deepfakes and published text (Art. 50(4))
**Image or video label, visible on the content:**
> AI-generated image / This video was created or altered with AI.

**Audio, at the start:**
> This audio was generated with AI.

**Creative work, for example in the credits or description:**
> Some scenes in this [film / advert] were generated or altered with AI.

**Text published to inform the public, unless a person reviewed it and someone holds editorial responsibility:**
> This article was generated with AI.
