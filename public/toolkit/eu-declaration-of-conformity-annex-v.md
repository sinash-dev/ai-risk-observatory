# EU declaration of conformity outline (EU AI Act Art. 47 and Annex V)

For **providers** of high-risk AI systems, after the conformity assessment. One declaration per system. It must be written, and may be machine-readable, physical or electronically signed. Keep it for 10 years after the system is placed on the market or put into service, translate it into a language the authorities of each Member State where the system is offered can easily understand, and keep it up to date (Art. 47(1), (2) and (4)).

If other EU product law also requires a declaration of conformity for the same system, draw up a single declaration that covers all of it and names each law (Art. 47(3)).

By signing, the provider takes responsibility for meeting the requirements of Chapter III, Section 2 (Art. 47(4)).
