# Korea AI Basic Act: user notices and labels (Art. 31)

For AI business operators that offer products or services using **high-impact AI** or **generative AI** to users in Korea. The Framework Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and the Establishment of a Foundation for Trust (the AI Basic Act) and its Enforcement Decree took effect on 22 January 2026.

> **Enforcement:** the Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) runs a guidance period of at least one year from that date, during which fact-finding investigations and fines are generally deferred, except in serious cases such as loss of life or human rights violations. Failing to give the advance notice in Art. 31(1) can lead to a fine of up to KRW 30 million (Art. 43).

> **Language:** write the notices in Korean for Korean users. The English below is for drafting only.

## The three duties in Art. 31
| Art. 31 | Applies to | Duty |
|---|---|---|
| (1) | Products or services using high-impact or generative AI | Tell users **in advance** that the product or service runs on AI |
| (2) | Generative AI products or services | **Label** outputs as generated by generative AI |
| (3) | AI outputs such as sound, images or video that are hard to tell apart from reality | Notify or label them in a way users can **clearly recognise**; artistic and creative works may be marked in a way that does not get in the way of enjoying them |

According to the MSIT transparency guidance as reported by Korean law firms, the duties do not apply where it is already obvious that a service is based on AI, or where AI is used only for internal business purposes. Simply putting "AI" in the product name may not be enough: users should be able to tell which part of the service uses generative AI. Check the current Enforcement Decree and MSIT guidelines for the accepted methods of notice and labelling for each content type.

## 1. Advance notice (Art. 31(1))
**Before first use, for example on the sign-up screen, the start screen or the product page:**
> [Service] uses [generative AI / AI that may affect important decisions about you] to [what it does]. [Which features use it.] [Answers and images it creates may be inaccurate.] More information: [link].

## 2. Output label (Art. 31(2))
**Next to or inside the generated output:**
> Generated by AI

For files, also record the machine-readable marking you use (metadata or watermark): [ ]

## 3. Realistic synthetic media (Art. 31(3))
**Shown clearly on the content, for example as an on-screen caption or a spoken statement at the start of audio:**
> This [image / video / voice] was created by AI and is not real.

## High-impact AI
If the service is high-impact AI (for example in hiring, credit decisions, healthcare or other areas listed in the Act), Art. 34 adds duties such as a risk management plan, explanation of results as far as technically possible, user protection, human management and oversight, and keeping records.
